Phase 1 · Online casino

Feasibility studyGo or no-go

Online casino legislation is moving in more states, and your property will have to take a position before the numbers are in. Support it, oppose it or prepare for it: each choice rests on the same unknowns.

How much floor revenue would online play take? How much new revenue would it add? And who keeps the margin, you or a platform partner? This study answers those questions for your property specifically, and we work at a fast pace, because the timing of the decision matters.

What the study answers

  1. Cannibalization. What share of current slot and table revenue is at risk, by player segment, based on comparable properties in states that already have online casino.
  2. New revenue. What online play could add, net of tax and platform costs, under the license terms proposed in your state.
  3. Structure. Whether to build, partner with a platform operator or lease the license, and what each choice does to your margin and control.
  4. Protection. How to tie online play to on-property rewards so the floor gains from it.
  5. Position. What to ask for in the legislation: tax rate, number of brands per license and promotional rules.

You receive a written report and a board-ready summary, ending with a clear recommendation and the conditions that would change it.

How the work runs

  • Your data. Player segments, floor revenue by game type, rewards structure and market radius. Everything stays under NDA.
  • The comparables. Monthly land-based and online revenue from every state with legal online casino, matched to properties like yours.
  • The model. Cannibalization and new-revenue scenarios under each structure, tested against tax rates and partner terms.
  • The recommendation. Written report, board summary and a working session with your team.

From you we need a data contact, two hours with your general manager and CFO, and one review meeting midway. How quickly we finish depends mostly on how fast your team can share the data.
