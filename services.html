Phase 1 · Online casino feasibility study

Online casino is coming to more states. Know your numbers before you take a position.

Support the legislation, oppose it or prepare for it: each choice rests on the same unknowns. This study answers them for your property specifically, and we work at a fast pace, because the timing of the decision matters.

Cannibalization

What share of slot and table revenue is at risk, by player segment, based on comparable properties in states that already have online casino.

New revenue

What online play could add, net of tax and platform costs, under the license terms proposed in your state.

Structure

Whether to build, partner with a platform operator or lease the license, and what each does to your margin and control.

Protection

How to tie online play to on-property rewards so the floor gains from it.

Position

What to ask for in the legislation: tax rate, brands per license and promotional rules.
